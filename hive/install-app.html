Put the Parent Portal on Your Phone

Open it with one tap, like an app

This is optional. It is not a download from an app store and it takes almost no space on your device. It simply puts a Kid City icon on your home screen that opens the Parent Portal. You can also skip this and use the link in our emails.
Use Safari. On an iPhone or iPad this only works in Safari. If you opened the link from an email or text and it opened inside another app, tap the Safari (compass) icon or "Open in Safari" first.
1
Open the Parent Portal link in Safari and log in.
2
Tap the Share button ⬆ (a square with an arrow pointing up). On an iPhone it is at the bottom of the screen. On an iPad it is at the top.
3
Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen. If you do not see it, tap View More first.
4
You can keep the name or change it to Kid City. Then tap Add in the top corner.
5
Done! Look for the green and gold Kid City icon on your home screen. Tap it any time to open the Parent Portal.
Use Chrome. If you opened the link inside another app, tap the three dots and choose "Open in Chrome" first.
1
Open the Parent Portal link in Chrome and log in.
2
Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top right corner.
3
Tap Add to Home screen. Some phones say Install app instead. Either one is right.
4
Tap Install or Add. If it asks where to put the icon, choose Add automatically or drop it on your home screen.
5
Done! Look for the green and gold Kid City icon. Tap it any time to open the Parent Portal.

Samsung phone or tablet using the Samsung Internet browser

1
Tap the menu ☰ at the bottom right.
2
Tap Add page to, then Home screen, then Add.
Good to know: For your child's safety the portal asks for the family password each time you open it. The password is in our weekly emails.
