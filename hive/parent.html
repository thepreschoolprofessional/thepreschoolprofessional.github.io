🐻 Bear Phonics — learn to read with Mrs. Bear!

B.E.A.R. Phonics (Bee Elite Academy of Reading) is Mrs. Bear's very own story-based reading program — the same one we use in our classrooms — and it's free for every Kid City USA family on YouTube. Join Claudia and Mrs. Bear on their listening adventures: your little bestie will hear each sound in a fun story, learn how their mouth makes it, and start blending sounds into real words and sentences. Children as young as three can learn to read! 💛
