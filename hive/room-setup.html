📲 Bear Classroom Pack (tablet app)

  1. On the classroom tablet, scan this code or open the page (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPad).
  2. Pick this tablet's classroom. It remembers it.
  3. Chrome: menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". Safari: Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap the "Classroom Pack" icon any time to open your room.

What each room page shows

Classroom cleaning chart, bathroom cleaning chart, daily indoor inspection and teacher bios, with the last 2 months of history. If today's chart is not done, the page shows a red note and a button to sign in and complete it. Lesson plans, schedules and drill logs are coming next. Sanford rooms come after DeLand 2.
