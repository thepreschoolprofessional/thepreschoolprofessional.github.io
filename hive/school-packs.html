School QR Packs

The whole poster pack for each school, all 13 pages. Tap a page to open it full size, print the whole pack with the Print button, or download the PDF. Hang each poster where its page says. Same codes every time; they never need reprinting.

Good to know

  1. The office, kitchen, visitor and classroom packs open only when the phone is at the school.
  2. The Director Pack opens anywhere with the director code (or the owner code) and gives access to everything. Keep that poster in the director office only.
  3. Every pack shows the last 2 months. A yellow NOT DONE day can be filled in on the spot and saves to the official form.
  4. Classroom packs include food program attendance and allergies.
